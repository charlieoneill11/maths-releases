Packing side-two cubes in the three-dimensional torus of side N≡5 mod 8

Charles O'Neill, Max Kirkby and Astra · 25 November 2026
PDFBibTeXPrograms and dataAll twelve papers

Abstract

Let N=4m+1 with m odd. We prove that the largest number of pairwise disjoint translates of the cube {0,1}3 in the discrete torus ℤN3 is N(mN-1)/2. Equivalently, the independence number of the third strong power of the cycle CN is α(CN⊠3)=N(mN-1)/2 for every N≡5 mod 8. The lower bound is a construction of Baumert, McEliece, Rodemich, Rumsey, Stanley and Taylor, who conjectured in 1971 that it is optimal. Bohman, Holzman and Natarajan proved the conjecture when N is prime and showed that in general the maximum exceeds the construction by at most two; Natarajan proved it when m is prime. We build on their analysis of full and almost-full planar projections. Arithmetic constraints between the hole lines of perpendicular full planes, a slope-propagation argument for adjacent almost-full planes and a new embedding for one case of their reduction exclude every side N≥653. Below 653, exact integer comparisons at 23 sides and exhaustive computations at the sides 85, 133, 141, 261 and 301 settle the remaining cases. The proof is computer-assisted, and all programs are available.
