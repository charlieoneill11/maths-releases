A Hadamard matrix of order 4792

Charles O'Neill, Max Kirkby and Astra · 25 November 2026
PDFBibTeXPrograms and dataAll ten papers

Abstract

We construct a Hadamard matrix of order 4792=8·599, an order for which no Hadamard matrix was previously known. The order is twice 2396, the smallest order for which no Hadamard matrix is known, and it was one of the last two open orders of the form 8p below 10 000. The matrix is a Goethals–Seidel array with a border, built from four ±1 functions on the abelian group ℤ7×ℤ19×𝔽9 of order 1197 whose periodic autocorrelations sum to -4 at every nonzero shift. The four functions are invariant under a group of multipliers of order 36, so they are given by twelve 17-bit masks. We verify the autocorrelation identity by counting differences, and we check that HHT=4792I with three independent programs. The functions were found by a computer search in a family of symmetric structures proposed by Kumar, who used the same construction to give a Hadamard matrix of order 5912=8·739.
