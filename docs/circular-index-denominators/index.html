Every denominator occurs in the upper half for the circular chromatic index

Charles O'Neill, Max Kirkby and Astra · 25 November 2026
PDFBibTeXPrograms and dataAll eleven papers

Abstract

Mazák and Zrubák asked whether for every integer q>2 there is a graph or multigraph G whose circular chromatic index is a fraction with reduced denominator q lying strictly between \Delta(G)+1 / (2) and \Delta(G)+1. We show that the answer is yes, and that G can be taken to be a connected regular simple graph of degree six or nine. For multigraphs a multiple of an odd cycle already works: (q-1)C2q+1 has circular chromatic index \Delta+1-1 / (q), which also follows from a theorem of Gao and Zhu. For simple graphs we prove that every rational number in [19 / (2),29 / (3)] is the circular chromatic index of a connected 9-regular simple graph. The graphs are obtained from cubic graphs: replacing every vertex by a hub carrying two copies of a fixed 13-vertex graph multiplies the circular chromatic index by three in this range, and Lukoťka and Mazák constructed cubic graphs with every rational circular chromatic index between 3 and 10 / (3). The interval contains fractions with every denominator q>2 except 4, 6 and 10, and for these three we give 6-regular graphs with circular chromatic indices 27 / (4), 41 / (6) and 67 / (10). The proofs are computer-assisted. The main finite step, about the 13-vertex graph, is an exhaustive search over 221 975 cases, carried out by two independently written programs.
