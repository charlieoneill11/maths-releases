# Three mixed nonadjacent cases

A 113-word four-dimensional packing cannot have all eight chosen full bands in any of the three mixed cases 2223, 2233 or 2333 when no axis has adjacent full starts. A full band has 33 words. Each digit gives the cyclic distance between the two marked starts on one axis.

Choose an axis with distance two, call it \(A\), and translate or reflect it so that its two marked bands have starts 0 and 2. Normalizing the projected support of the first band can permute the three remaining coordinates. For this reason the replay tests all three placements of the exceptional distance in 2223, all three in 2233 and the one placement 333 in 2333. On each transverse axis, it tries every first start modulo seven, so either cyclic ordering of an unordered pair is covered by these translated positive-distance pairs.

The union of these seven ordered tests retains 16,076,232 of 16,939,304,704 ordered source-support pairs after matching the necessary planar crossings. A pair can survive in more than one ordered test. The second filter checks which of the six transverse bands can give consistent heights on the two distinguished supports. In particular, the middle \(A\)-band at levels 1 and 2 must contain at least 31 words. To see this, write \(d_i=33-|B_i|\). Then \(d_0=d_2=0\) and \(\sum_i d_i=5\). The no-adjacency premise gives \(d_1,d_3,d_6\ge1\) and \(d_4+d_5\ge1\), so \(d_1\le2\). The final join tries the actual 33-word projections of all six transverse bands, enforcing their planar intersections and the physical height constraints on the distinguished bands. No complete eight-band choice survives. [Shared proof and replay](README.md)

The published replay tests 96,313,808 placements and retains 17,702 layouts across 15,051 source pairs. A layout specifies the two source supports and a start and separation on each of the other three axes. The six-band join visits 166,543 compatible prefixes. A separate implementation reconstructs the 33-word projections from their coordinates and tests all 17,702 layouts. Exactly one layout admits six transverse projections satisfying every planar overlap. Its physical heights are inconsistent. [Independent coordinate audit](audit_projections.cpp)

The per-pattern census follows the published convention of choosing the least-numbered motion producing a given right-hand support. A support with a nontrivial symmetry may be produced by several motions. Changing the representative can reorder its words and cause the augmenting-path algorithm to select another perfect matching of the same bipartite conflict graph. Every physical middle band leaves at most two edges empty in **every** such matching. Testing any one matching therefore retains every feasible layout, although the set of infeasible layouts passing this intermediate test can change. An earlier ordering retained 17,701 layouts. All counts in the table use the deterministic 17,702-layout replay. [Seven-pattern census](mixed-profiles.tsv)
